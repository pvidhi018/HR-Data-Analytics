# HR Data Analytics – Employee Attendance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attendance data over multiple months to help HR understand workplace attendance patterns, work-from-home (WFH) preferences, and sick leave trends. The dataset includes attendance statuses such as Present, Work From Home, Sick Leave, Paid Half Day, and Unpaid Half Day.

The interactive dashboard transforms attendance records into meaningful insights, helping HR make informed decisions about office planning, meeting schedules, resource allocation, and employee attendance trends.

## 🎯 Business Problem
HR has monthly attendance sheets containing information about employee presence, work-from-home days, sick leaves, and half-day leaves. However, analyzing raw spreadsheets makes it difficult to identify patterns and make timely decisions.

HR needs answers to questions such as:
- Which days of the week have the highest work-from-home attendance?
- How frequently do employees work from home?
- Which days have the highest number of employees present in the office?
- How can HR choose suitable days for meetings and collaborative activities?
- When would office maintenance be least disruptive?
- Are sick leaves increasing during particular months or seasons?
- How can attendance patterns support office space planning and cost optimization?

## 💡 Project Solution
An interactive HR analytics dashboard was developed to summarize attendance data and highlight employee attendance patterns.

The dashboard allows HR to:
- Monitor the percentage of employees present, working from home, and taking sick leave.
- Filter attendance insights by month using interactive buttons.
- Review individual employee attendance, including present days, WFH days, and sick days.
- Analyze the days on which employees are present in the office.
- Identify days with higher work-from-home activity.
- Explore monthly attendance patterns to support HR planning and decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- **Attendance Overview:** Percentage of employees present, working from home, and on sick leave.
- **Monthly Analysis:** Interactive month selection to explore attendance trends for a specific month.
- **Employee Attendance Table:** Employee-level breakdown of present days, WFH days, and sick days.
- **Office Presence Analysis:** Table showing the days employees are present in the office.
- **Work-From-Home Analysis:** Table highlighting employees' WFH days and patterns.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel – Attendance data
- Microsoft Power BI – Interactive dashboard and data visualization

## 📈 Business Value
The dashboard can help HR teams:
- Schedule meetings on days when more employees are present in the office.
- Plan maintenance and office activities during periods of lower office attendance.
- Understand work-from-home patterns to support workplace planning.
- Monitor sick leave trends and investigate possible seasonal patterns.
- Evaluate office space requirements and potential cost savings while maintaining productivity.

*Note: Attendance patterns can highlight trends, but they do not establish the cause of sick leave or prove that reduced office space would be appropriate. HR should validate these decisions with additional information.*

## 📝 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how employee attendance data can be transformed into actionable HR insights using Power BI. By replacing manual spreadsheet reviews with an interactive dashboard, HR teams can explore attendance trends more efficiently and make better-informed decisions about workforce coordination, office operations, and resource planning.
